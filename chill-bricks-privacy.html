Chill Bricks – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how the Chill Bricks mobile game ("the game") handles information. The game is made by samgame.

1. Information we do not collect

The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and it has no user accounts.

2. Information stored on your device

Your best score, settings, unlocked skins and saved game are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

3. Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions and crash diagnostics.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in the game's settings. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings.

Learn how Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Rewards

Rewards in the game, such as revives and skins, have no cash value.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
